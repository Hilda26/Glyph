# Design Notes

MotionSites was inspected before implementing the UI. Relevant references:

- `Digital Epoch`: strong artifact-forward hero framing and page rhythm.
- `Impressive Hero`: large headline with a kinetic visual object in first viewport.
- `Shamoni`: typography-led composition with confident contrast.

Glyphwork does not copy assets, text, logos, or layouts. The design translates the motion/layout principles into a restoration desk:

- vellum and paper surfaces;
- archival black and registry blue for contrast;
- brass and faded green as secondary marks;
- accession numbers and stamps;
- source image as the dominant workbench object;
- split-screen transcription flow.
